# Stage 4 — Repair

## Delivered
- Intake with customer, device, IMEI, condition, accessories, issue, and attachment model.
- Controlled status timeline from intake through delivery.
- Diagnosis, estimate, explicit approval/rejection, and technician assignment.
- Parts from stock, dedicated purchase, or external source.
- Stock part consumption produces an append-only REPAIR_USE movement.
- Deposits and partial payments with historical FX snapshot.
- Repair Profit Truth: parts, labor, direct cost, final price, profit, and balance.
- Delivery timestamps and repair warranty issuance.
- Tenant/branch isolation and audit events for sensitive transitions and financial truth.
