# Stage 6 — Cash / Currency

## Delivered
- One open cash session per branch.
- Opening balances in USD and LBP with opening FX snapshot.
- Append-only cash movements for sales, repairs, debt, expenses, purchases, deposits, withdrawals, refunds, and adjustments.
- Historical currency/rate/base amount on every movement.
- Expected-versus-physical daily close.
- Mandatory variance reason and approver.
- Owner-set exchange rate history and audit events.
