# A1 Mobi — +1 commercial competitor evidence (2026-10-25)

Source scope: official competitor product pages checked 2026-10-25; claims are vendor-advertised, not independently feature-tested. Prior named comparator Mobiqore page https://mobiqore.com/ opened but its public text could not be extracted, so no invented line-by-line comparison with it.

## Verified competitor offers

- **CellStore** official https://www.cellstore.co/: advertises POS, sale/payment tracking, IMEI and serial stock, repair tickets, customers, multiple locations, device-responsive web access, receipt printers/scanners/cash drawers, Excel/CSV/TXT imports, marketing/CRM, warranty and business insights. Its home page advertises **US$39/month** and a 14-day free trial (public offer checked on this date; pricing subject to change).
- **RepairShopr** official https://www.repairshopr.com/features: advertises integrated POS, barcode stock, tickets, customer CRM, customer self-service portal and estimate approvals, SMS/email notifications, parts procurement linked to repair tickets, deposits, invoices, recurring billing, RMA, CSV import, marketing automation, multi-register workflow and warranty-linked serial tracking.

## Our local evidence, without premature superiority claims

A1 Mobi has verified local database-backed tenant/branch isolation, real owner auth, scans and barcode labels, POS sale/return, stock count, audit, C14 independent cash approval, customer/repair routes and backup restore (gate details: `docs/reviews/2026-10-25/SEVEN-PLUS-ONE-CLOSURE-PROGRESS.md`). A/B/C and RTL/EN positioning are product intent; screen-level owner acceptance remains open. Do not claim native physical device support from Chrome simulated scans or print PDFs.

## Commercial decision for a sellable pilot

1. **P0 to show the customer:** 15-minute real owner walk-through: first login, configure branch, add a product/model, goods receipt, checkout with scanner, return, repair ticket/status, customer update, cash/day close, reports, backup/restore confidence; AR and EN. Any unusable step blocks customer handoff.
2. **P0 parity:** physical scanner and 80×40mm printer calibration, printable receipt, minimum-price override permissions, practical stock/IMEI path and complete inventory import proof. Competitor sites explicitly market these essentials; we need direct business acceptance.
3. **P1 meaningful differentiation:** Arabic-first RTL clarity, intuitive one-screen owner operations, repair+retail combined and transparent pricing/service—not an unevidenced claim of more features.
4. **P1 market gap review:** customer-facing repair tracking and estimate approval, proactive SMS/WhatsApp progress alerts, parts-to-repair linkage and warranty history. Confirm priorities with MobiLine customer before adding scope; these are capabilities marketed by RepairShopr/CellStore, not all proven missing from our code.
5. **P2 later:** built-in CRM campaigns and advanced multi-store analytics; defer until first paid operator uses core workflows reliably.
